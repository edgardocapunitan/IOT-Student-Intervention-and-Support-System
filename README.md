# IOT-Student-Intervention-and-Support-System
IOT:Student Intervention and Support System for Student Who Need Area Of Improvements
Download the File and Extract it
Create a Database Named 'Finals' then import the sql file
Must have a RFID READER
1.1.	Tap the RFID Card for Time In. To mark your attendance, tap your RFID card on the system to register the time you arrive. 
1.2.	Tap the RFID Card for Time Out. When leaving, tap your RFID card again to mark your time out.
2.1. Go to Admin personnel to sign up and create an account. Fill in the required information and click Submit to complete the registration process.
2.2. After signing up, click Teacher Log In. Upon successful login, you will be directed to the Student Management menu.
2.3. Click the Manage Student button to access options to add, delete, edit, search, and view 
2.4. Click the Grading System button to input grades for students. The system will automatically identify any Failed Subjects and display the student’s current Status.
2.5. Click the Intervention button to proceed. Select a student whose subject has been identified as an Area of Improvement by the system. Click on the student’s information to open their record. Input the necessary intervention details. After completing the inputs, click Submit to save the details. The information will be saved in the My Records interface.
2.6. To go back to the Student Management Menu, click the Back button. Then, click the Student Status button to review student progress.
2.7. Click the Send Email button to notify students whose subject is marked for Area of Improvement. This email will inform the student to cooperate with the teacher. After the intervention is completed, mark the student’s status as Completed in the Intervention Status field.
